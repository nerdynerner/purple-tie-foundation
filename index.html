Our Programs

Education, expression, and opportunity.

Rural Youth Scholarship

We award annual scholarships to Grade 12 students in Strathcona County, with applications open from January to March and awards given in April. Beyond this, we also organize annual essay writing and debate contests for 200 students across 20 public schools in Ikot Ekpene, Nigeria.

School Outreach Visits

Through our school outreach visits, we deliver anti-racism and mental health workshops to students in Grades 7–9. These programs are designed to equip young people for future challenges and encourage their active involvement in the community.

Cultural Awareness

We host Youth Leadership Forums and other events focused on cultural awareness. Our goal is to foster understanding and appreciation for diverse cultures, promoting a more inclusive environment.

Mental Wellness Workshops

In collaboration with guidance counsellors, we provide in-class presentations as part of our mental wellness workshops. These sessions offer health education and resources supporting the well-being of young individuals.

Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria

Building systems young people can return to as they grow.

Each year, Purple Tie Foundation brings together students from over 20 government secondary schools across Akwa Ibom State. Students are challenged to think critically and articulate their ideas in front of peers, educators, and community members.

In Season 6 alone, ₦4.3 million was raised and invested toward students' education, and desktop computers were donated to 62 schools across the state.
