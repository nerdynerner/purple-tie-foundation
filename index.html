Where we work

Local roots. International experience.

Purple Tie operates across two distinct contexts. In Alberta, our work includes rural scholarships and school-based youth programming. In Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria, our annual essay writing and debate competitions bring together students from government secondary schools across the region.

Impact in practice

Six seasons of student voices.

Since 2021, Purple Tie’s annual essay writing and debate competitions have created a recurring platform for students to develop ideas, speak publicly and compete for educational support.

In Season 6, ₦4.3 million was raised and invested toward students’ education, and desktop computers were donated to 62 schools across Akwa Ibom State.

How we work

Community-rooted and accountable.

Youth-centred

We design programs around the experiences, ambitions and barriers young people face.

Community-rooted

Schools, families, volunteers and local partners are part of how programs are delivered and sustained.

Access-focused

We focus on practical barriers to education, participation and opportunity across rural, urban and underserved communities.

Evidence-aware

We use participation data, feedback and program outcomes to strengthen future delivery.

Accountability

Built to earn trust.

Purple Tie Foundation is a federally incorporated Canadian nonprofit. We are strengthening our public governance and reporting infrastructure as the organization grows.
