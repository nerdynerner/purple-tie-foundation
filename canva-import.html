Nigeria

Akwa Ibom State

Essay writing and debate competitions, educational investment, and school technology support.

Six seasons of student voices

Since 2021, Purple Tie’s annual essay writing and debate competitions have created a recurring platform for students to develop ideas, speak publicly, and compete for educational support.

Season 6: ₦4.3 million raised and invested toward students’ education, with desktop computers donated to 62 schools across Akwa Ibom State.
