About Purple Tie

Young people deserve access to opportunity and a real role in shaping their communities.

Purple Tie Foundation is a Canadian nonprofit working across education, youth leadership, well-being and community opportunity.

Our mission

Education, expression and opportunity.

We invest in young people through practical programs that expand access to education, strengthen confidence and create opportunities to participate in community life.

Our work has grown across Alberta and Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria, while staying focused on a common idea: young people should have the tools, support and opportunity to contribute meaningfully to the places they call home.

Our story

From a student competition to a growing youth organization.

2021

Purple Tie launches its first annual essay writing competition in Akwa Ibom State.

2022–2025

The competition grows across multiple seasons while Purple Tie expands community and school-based initiatives.

2026

Season 6 continues the annual competition tradition, alongside scholarship, school outreach and youth programming in Alberta.
