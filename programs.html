Our work

Our programs

Our current work spans education, school outreach, youth leadership, cultural awareness and mental wellness.

01

Education & Opportunity

Education & Opportunity

Rural Youth Scholarship

Annual scholarships support Grade 12 students in Strathcona County. Applications run from January to March, with awards presented in April.

Essay Writing & Debate Competitions

Each year, students from government secondary schools across Akwa Ibom State gather to think critically, write, debate and compete for educational support.

02

Youth Leadership & Community

Youth Leadership & Community

Cultural Awareness

Youth leadership forums and community events create space for young people to learn across differences and contribute to more inclusive communities.

Community Participation

Our programming encourages young people to build confidence, articulate their ideas and take active roles in the communities around them.

03

Well-being & Belonging

Well-being & Belonging

School Outreach Visits

Anti-racism and mental-health workshops for students in Grades 7–9 help young people build practical skills and confidence for future challenges.

Mental Wellness Workshops

In collaboration with guidance counsellors, in-class presentations connect students with health education and resources that support well-being.
