Where we work

Different contexts. One commitment to youth opportunity.

Purple Tie works in Alberta and Akwa Ibom State through programs shaped around local needs and community relationships.

Canada

Alberta

Our Alberta work includes rural scholarships, school outreach, anti-racism education, mental wellness programming, youth leadership and community engagement.

  • Rural Youth Scholarship in Strathcona County
  • School outreach for students in Grades 7–9
  • Mental wellness and anti-racism workshops
  • Youth leadership and community programming

Nigeria

Akwa Ibom State

Our work in Akwa Ibom State centres on education, confidence and student opportunity, including an annual essay writing and debate competition involving government secondary schools across the region.

  • Six seasons of annual competitions
  • Students from more than 20 government secondary schools
  • ₦4.3 million invested toward students’ education in Season 6
  • Desktop computers donated to 62 schools in Season 6

Why place matters

Programs should make sense where they operate.

Our Canadian and Nigerian programs are not copies of one another. Each responds to a different community context while advancing the same broader goal: helping young people access education, build confidence and participate in shaping their communities.
